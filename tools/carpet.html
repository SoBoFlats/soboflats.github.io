Common Questions
What is RERA Carpet Area?›
  • RERA Carpet Area is the net usable floor area within an apartment, as defined by the Real Estate Regulation Act (2016)
  • Includes: area enclosed within walls, internal partition walls, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms
  • Excludes: external walls, areas under service shafts, exclusive balcony or verandah, exclusive open terrace
  • This is the standard across all states — MahaRERA, UP-RERA, KRERA etc. all follow the same definition
  • All builders must now quote RERA Carpet Area — it’s the only legally comparable metric
What is the difference between Carpet, Built-up and Super Built-up?›
  • RERA Carpet Area — What you walk on. Net usable area inside the apartment. RERA standard.
  • Built-up Area — Carpet + external wall thickness + balcony + verandah. Typically 10–20% more than carpet.
  • Super Built-up / Saleable Area — Built-up + proportionate share of common areas (lobby, stairs, lift, gym, corridors). Typically 25–45% more than carpet.

Key insight: Two apartments with the same “1,500 sq ft super built-up” can have very different carpet areas depending on the loading factor. Always compare on carpet.

What was “pre-RERA Carpet Area”?›
  • Before RERA, builders had no standardised carpet area definition
  • Some included balconies, dry balconies, flower beds, and even half of enclosed verandahs in “carpet area”
  • This made carpet areas look 5–10% larger than the actual usable space
  • Post-RERA, the definition is uniform — excludes all of the above
  • If comparing old property documents with new RERA carpet, expect the old number to be slightly higher
What is “loading” in Mumbai real estate?›
  • Loading is the percentage difference between carpet area and super built-up area
  • It covers walls, balconies, lobbies, staircases, lifts, and all amenity areas
  • SoBo new buildings: 30–35% loading is typical (premium lobbies, larger common areas)
  • Suburban buildings: 25–30% loading
  • Ultra luxury: Can go up to 40–45% (private lifts, grand lobbies, podium parking)
  • Tip: A “lower loading” is better for the buyer — more carpet per rupee spent
How to calculate effective price per carpet sq ft?›
  • Most builders quote rate per super built-up sq ft
  • Effective carpet rate = (Rate per SBU sq ft × Super Built-up Area) ÷ RERA Carpet Area
  • Example: ₹25,000/SBU sq ft at 35% loading → effective carpet rate = ₹33,750/carpet sq ft
  • Use the Price Impact section above to calculate this instantly
  • Always compare properties on carpet rate — two buildings quoting ₹25K/sq ft can differ by ₹5,000+/carpet sq ft due to different loading
Is MahaRERA Carpet same as all-state RERA Carpet?›
  • Yes. RERA 2016 is a central act — the carpet area definition is the same nationwide
  • MahaRERA (Maharashtra), UP-RERA, KRERA (Karnataka), TNRERA (Tamil Nadu) etc. all follow the same section 2(k) definition
  • The only variation is in how strictly each state enforces the disclosure, not in the definition itself
  • This tool uses the uniform RERA definition for all calculations
