Common Questions
What is stamp duty?›
  • Stamp duty is a tax levied by state governments on property transactions
  • It serves as legal evidence of the property purchase and is mandatory
  • Rates vary by state, property type, buyer gender and property value
  • Must be paid before or at the time of registration
  • Calculated on the higher of: agreement value or ready reckoner / circle rate
Is stamp duty different for men and women?›
  • Yes — many states offer 1–2% lower stamp duty for women buyers
  • Maharashtra: Women pay 5% vs 6% for men
  • Delhi: Women pay 4% vs 6% for men
  • Rajasthan: Women pay 4% vs 5% for men
  • Punjab & Haryana: Women pay 5% vs 7% for men
  • Joint ownership with a woman may qualify for reduced rates in some states
When is GST applicable on property?›
  • Under-construction properties: 5% GST (without input tax credit)
  • Affordable housing (under-construction): 1% GST — property value ≤ ₹45L in metro cities, ₹30L in non-metro
  • Ready-to-move properties with OC: No GST
  • Metro cities for GST: Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad
  • GST is calculated on full agreement value (not just construction portion)
  • Stamp duty and GST are separate charges — both apply on under-construction
What is metro cess?›
  • Metro cess is an additional 1% surcharge to fund metro rail construction
  • In Maharashtra, it applies in Pune, Thane, Nagpur and Navi Mumbai
  • Mumbai’s 6% stamp duty already includes metro cess — no additional charge
  • This is over and above regular stamp duty and registration fees
  • Calculated on the same value as stamp duty (agreement or ready reckoner, whichever is higher)
What are builder extra charges?›
  • PLC (Preferential Location Charges) — For corner units, garden-facing, higher floors
  • Infrastructure charges — For amenities like clubhouse, gym, pool
  • Development charges — Builder’s infrastructure development costs
  • Legal & documentation — Agreement drafting, advocate fees
  • Club house / maintenance deposit — Advance maintenance for 1–2 years
  • Typical total: 1–4% above the base property price
  • Always ask for a complete cost sheet before booking — these charges add up
Can I claim tax benefit on stamp duty?›
  • Section 80C — Stamp duty and registration charges deductible up to ₹1.5 lakh (old regime)
  • Deduction available only in the year of purchase
  • Combined limit with other 80C investments (PPF, ELSS, LIC, etc.)
  • New tax regime does not offer this deduction
  • Both stamp duty AND registration fee qualify

What documents are needed for registration?›
  • Sale Agreement / Sale Deed (stamped and signed)
  • ID proof of buyer and seller (Aadhaar, PAN mandatory)
  • Photographs — Passport-size photos of both parties
  • 2 Witnesses with ID proof
  • Previous chain documents — Prior sale deeds, allotment letter
  • Property card / 7/12 extract (for land in Maharashtra)
  • NOC from society (for resale flats)
  • Challan of stamp duty + registration fee payment
