Common Questions
What is the most common unit in Mumbai real estate?›
  • Square Feet (sq ft) is the standard unit in Mumbai and all urban Indian real estate
  • Carpet area, built-up area and super built-up area are all quoted in sq ft
  • RERA mandates carpet area disclosure in sq ft for all projects
  • For land parcels in rural Maharashtra, Guntha and Acre are more common
What is a Guntha?›
  • Guntha (also spelled Gunta) is used mainly in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh
  • In Maharashtra: 1 Guntha = 1,089 sq ft (33 ft × 33 ft)
  • 40 Gunthas = 1 Acre
  • Commonly used for agricultural land and NA (non-agricultural) plots
  • 7/12 extracts and property cards in Maharashtra quote area in Gunthas or Hectares
Why does Bigha vary by state?›

Bigha is a traditional land measurement that was never standardised nationally:

  • Rajasthan — 1 Bigha = 27,225 sq ft (largest)
  • UP / Bihar — 1 Bigha = ~27,000 sq ft
  • Gujarat — 1 Bigha = 17,424 sq ft
  • West Bengal / Assam — 1 Bigha = 14,400 sq ft
  • Madhya Pradesh — 1 Bigha = 12,000 sq ft
  • Uttarakhand — 1 Bigha = 6,804 sq ft (smallest)

Always verify the local standard before any transaction.

What is the difference between Carpet, Built-up and Super Built-up area?›
  • Carpet Area — Usable floor area within the walls. What you actually walk on. RERA standard.
  • Built-up Area — Carpet + wall thickness + balcony. Typically 10–15% more than carpet.
  • Super Built-up Area — Built-up + share of common areas (lobby, stairs, gym etc.). Typically 25–40% more than carpet.
  • Loading factor — The % added for common areas. In SoBo, typically 30–35% for new buildings.
  • Tip: Always ask for carpet area when comparing properties. Super built-up varies widely between builders.
How are plots measured in Maharashtra?›
  • Urban plots — Square Feet or Square Meters
  • Agricultural land — Gunthas, Acres, or Hectares
  • Revenue records (7/12, property card) — Usually in Hectares and Ares (1 Are = 100 sq m)
  • NA plots — Guntha is most common. Example: "5 Guntha NA plot" = 5,445 sq ft
  • Old documents may use Square Yards — common in pre-1990s SoBo property deeds
What are Kanal, Marla and Biswa?›

These are regional units used mainly in North India:

  • Kanal — Used in Punjab, Haryana, J&K. 1 Kanal = 5,445 sq ft. 8 Kanals = 1 Acre.
  • Marla — Sub-unit of Kanal. 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft. 20 Marlas = 1 Kanal.
  • Biswa — Used in UP, MP, Rajasthan. Size varies: 1 Biswa = 1,350 sq ft (UP) to 600 sq ft (MP).
  • Katha — Used in Bihar, Bengal, Assam. 1 Katha = 1,361 sq ft (Bihar) vs 720 sq ft (Bengal).
