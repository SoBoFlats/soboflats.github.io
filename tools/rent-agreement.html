Common Questions
How is stamp duty calculated for rent agreements?›
What is a Leave & License agreement?›
  • Leave & License is the standard rental agreement format in Maharashtra
  • The landlord (licensor) grants the tenant (licensee) permission to use the property for a fixed period
  • Maximum tenure is 60 months (5 years). Commonly 11 months
  • Must be registered with the Sub-Registrar office — unregistered agreements have no legal standing
  • The licensee does not get any ownership or tenancy rights — this protects the landlord
Who pays the agreement charges?›
  • Common practice: Stamp duty & registration split equally (50:50) between landlord and tenant
  • There is no legal mandate on who pays — it’s a matter of negotiation
  • In premium properties, landlords often cover all charges as a goodwill gesture
What documents are needed for registration?›
  • Aadhaar card of both landlord and tenant (original + photocopy)
  • PAN card of both parties
  • Passport-size photographs (2 each for landlord and tenant)
  • 2 witnesses with their Aadhaar & photos
  • Property documents: Index-II / ownership proof / society NOC
  • Stamp paper of appropriate value (physical or e-stamp)
  • Typed agreement draft with all terms and conditions
What if tenant is from outside India (NRI/USA)?›
  • Agreement registration process is the same for NRI tenants
  • Passport serves as identity proof (instead of Aadhaar for the foreign party)
  • Registration officer visit charges are higher for international locations
  • Power of Attorney can be given if the party cannot be physically present
  • Police verification is still mandatory and may involve FRRO registration
Is online (e-registration) available?›
  • Yes — Maharashtra offers online L&L registration via igrmaharashtra.gov.in
  • E-stamp can be purchased online through SHCIL or Stock Holding
  • Both parties need Aadhaar-linked biometric verification
  • Most advocates handle end-to-end online registration for a service fee
  • Online registration is legally equivalent to physical registration
What is police verification?›
  • Mandatory in Maharashtra for all rental agreements
  • Landlord must submit tenant details to the local police station within 1 month
  • Can be done online via the Tenant Information System (TIS) in some cities
  • Failure to inform police can result in a fine of up to ₹5,000
