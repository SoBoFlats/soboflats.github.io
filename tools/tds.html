How to Deposit TDS
Step-by-step process for tenants
1
Deduct TDS from Rent
Deduct the TDS amount before paying rent to your landlord. Keep the landlord’s PAN handy.
2
File Form 26QC Online
Visit the Income Tax e-filing portal. Fill Form 26QC with rent details, landlord PAN, and TDS amount. Due within 30 days from the end of the month in which TDS was deducted.
3
Pay TDS via Challan
Pay the TDS amount to the government via net banking or authorized bank. You’ll receive a challan receipt as proof.
4
Issue TDS Certificate (Form 16C)
Download Form 16C from TRACES portal and share with your landlord. The landlord uses this to claim TDS credit in their ITR.
Key Rules to Remember
Don’t miss these important points
📌 TDS is deducted from last month’s rent of the FY (or last payment if mid-year)
📌 No TDS certificate (Form 16A/16C) needed from the tenant — it’s auto-generated
📌 Late deposit penalty: 1.5% per month on TDS amount
📌 Late filing penalty: ₹200/day up to TDS amount
📌 Tenant does NOT need a TAN (Tax Deduction Account Number) for 194-IB
Common Questions
What is TDS on rent?›
  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is a mechanism where the payer deducts tax before making a payment
  • For rent, the tenant deducts TDS and deposits it with the government on behalf of the landlord
  • The landlord can claim this TDS as credit while filing their income tax return
  • Section 194-IB: Applies to individual/HUF tenants paying rent > ₹50,000/month
  • Section 194-I: Applies to business entities paying rent > ₹2,40,000/year
When does TDS on rent apply?›
  • Individual/HUF (194-IB): Monthly rent exceeds ₹50,000 — TDS at 2%
  • Business entities (194-I): Annual rent exceeds ₹2,40,000 — TDS at 10% for buildings
  • No PAN: Rate increases to 20% if the landlord doesn’t provide PAN
  • TDS applies on the total rent amount (not just the portion above the threshold)
  • Multiple properties: Threshold is per landlord, not per property
What is the difference between 194-I and 194-IB?›
  • Section 194-I: For business deductors (companies, firms, individuals/HUF whose books are audited)
  • Section 194-IB: For individual/HUF tenants whose books are NOT required to be audited
  • 194-I rate: 10% for building/furniture rent, 2% for plant/machinery
  • 194-IB rate: 2% (flat rate for all types)
  • 194-I threshold: ₹2,40,000 per year. 194-IB threshold: ₹50,000 per month
  • 194-I: Quarterly filing (Form 26Q), TAN required. 194-IB: Annual filing (Form 26QC), no TAN needed
Can the landlord ask me not to deduct TDS?›
  • No — TDS deduction is a legal obligation of the tenant (deductor)
  • The tenant is liable for penalties if TDS is not deducted when applicable
  • Even if the landlord says their income is below taxable limit, TDS must still be deducted
  • The landlord can file their ITR and claim a refund of the TDS if their total income is below the taxable slab
  • Landlord’s remedy: Apply for a lower deduction certificate (Section 197) from the AO
What if I forgot to deduct TDS?›
  • Interest on late deduction: 1% per month from the date TDS was deductible to the date of actual deduction
  • Interest on late deposit: 1.5% per month from deduction date to actual deposit date
  • Late filing fee: ₹200 per day (Section 234E) until TDS return is filed, capped at TDS amount
  • Penalty: Equal to TDS amount under Section 271H (minimum ₹10,000, maximum ₹1,00,000)
  • Disallowance: 30% of rent paid will be disallowed as business expense (for 194-I non-compliance)
  • Best approach: Deduct and deposit immediately with interest — voluntary compliance reduces penalty risk
How to file Form 26QC?›
  • Visit incometax.gov.in → e-File → e-Pay Tax → Form 26QC
  • Enter your PAN, landlord’s PAN, property address, rent period, and TDS amount
  • Pay TDS via net banking, debit card, or at authorized bank
  • After payment, download the challan receipt
  • Within 15 days of filing 26QC, download Form 16C from TRACES
  • Form 26QC is due within 30 days from the end of the month in which TDS was deducted
  • No TAN is required — you file using your PAN directly
Is TDS applicable on commercial rent?›
  • Yes — TDS applies to both residential and commercial property rent
  • For commercial rent paid by a business: Section 194-I at 10%
  • For commercial rent paid by an individual: Section 194-IB at 2% (if > ₹50,000/month)
  • GST: Commercial rent also attracts 18% GST if landlord is GST-registered. TDS is on rent excluding GST
  • Plant & machinery rent: TDS at 2% under 194-I (lower rate than building rent)
